Give Ripple a campaign goal, and I research the company's recent launches and posts, then create a brief for its main audience segment. The current copy worker writes two full posts in the brand's voice. Images, per-draft videos, edits, testing, and approval share the same saved campaign as the Ripple website.

Talk to **@ripple** in ASI:One and choose **Research company** or **Generate campaign**. Continue with **Test both drafts**, **Make campaign videos**, and **Approve A/B** in chat. Publishing happens in X's composer after you review the approved post.
